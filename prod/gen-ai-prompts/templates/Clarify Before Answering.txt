Tips for Using the "Clarify Before Answering"
Provide Context Always give as much context as possible in your initial question to minimize the need for clarifications.
Be Specific When modifying the prompt, tailor it to the specific needs of the task at hand, including relevant details.
Review Clarifications Pay close attention to the AI's clarifying questions to ensure all necessary information is provided.
Iterate as Needed Don't hesitate to refine your prompt based on the AI's questions to achieve the most accurate and useful response.
By following this guide, you can effectively use the Clarify Before Answering prompt to obtain precise, context-aware, and relevant answers from AI.
