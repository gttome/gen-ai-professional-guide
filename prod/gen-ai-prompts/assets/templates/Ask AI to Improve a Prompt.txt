Ask AI to Improve a Prompt: Prompt Engineering Resource

PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "Ask AI to Improve a Prompt" technique enables the user to leverage the AI's understanding of its own processing logic to refine a draft question. By asking the AI to critique and revise an initial input, the user transforms broad or vague requests into specific, actionable instructions, ensuring the final response is highly relevant to the actual requirements.

Syntax:
What changes or revisions can be made to this prompt to make your response better. Prompt = [Draft Prompt].

Usage Instructions:
Draft Prompt: Provide your initial, rough attempt at the question or task (e.g., "Tell me about the market").
The Meta-Prompt: Prepend the specific command "What changes or revisions..." to force the AI into an editorial role.
Iterative Refinement: Use the AI's feedback to finalize the prompt before executing the actual task.
