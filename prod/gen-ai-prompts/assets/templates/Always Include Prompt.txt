Using the Always Include
The Always Include prompt is a strategic tool designed to append specific content or actions at the end of every AI response. This ensures consistent and enhanced communication by providing additional support or information relevant to the user's needs. Whether it's offering further resources, suggesting next steps, or facilitating follow-up actions, this prompt aims to optimize user engagement and satisfaction.
Why
Consistency
Ensure that every response ends with a relevant addition, creating reliable user experience.
Relevance
Tailors the additional content or actions to the context of the conversation, making it more useful and actionable.
Engagement
Encourages ongoing interaction by suggesting the next steps or additional actions.
How
The prompt works by specifying what should be included at the beginning / end of the response, ensuring the AI provides a comprehensive and useful beginning or conclusion to the user's inquiry.

Step-by-Step Guide
Identify the Context
Understand the scenario where you want to use the "Always Include" prompt. Is it for customer service, education, or another purpose?
Specify the Content
Determine what additional content or action should be included at the end of the AI response. This could be links, suggestions, questions, or actions.

Review and Refine
Check the prompt for clarity and completeness. Ensure that it covers all necessary aspects and provides a consistent, relevant, and engaging conclusion to the AI response.
