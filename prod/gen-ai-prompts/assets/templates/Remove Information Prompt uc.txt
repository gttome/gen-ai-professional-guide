Remove Information Prompt

USE CASES

The following examples demonstrate the "Remove Information" methodology applied to professional scenarios requiring data privacy, brevity, or confidentiality.

ANONYMIZING LEGAL CASE SUMMARIES


Scenario:
A Law Firm needs to publish case studies for marketing but must protect client confidentiality and attorney-client privilege.

Prompt:
Thoroughly review the provided case summaries and remove any content specified in the brackets, including [client names, specific settlement amounts, and opposing counsel details]. Focus on the legal arguments and outcomes. Double-check the result to confirm no unintended content has been removed or retained.

REDACTING HEALTHCARE PATIENT FEEDBACK


Scenario:
A Hospital Administrator wants to share positive patient testimonials on a public website but must strip all PHI (Protected Health Information).

Prompt:
Thoroughly review the provided testimonials and remove any content specified in the brackets, including [personally identifying information, specific diagnosis details, and dates of service] or [details that could potentially be used to re-identify the person]. Rewrite the text as minimally as possible to maintain clarity while addressing issues caused by the removal.
