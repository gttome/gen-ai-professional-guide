Refine and Focus a Question Prompt

PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "Refine and Focus a Question" prompt is a pre-processing technique used to upgrade the quality of an inquiry before it is answered. By instructing the AI to rewrite a broad or vague question into a version that explicitly targets multiple specific dimensions, users ensure that the eventual answer will be comprehensive, nuanced, and structurally sound.

Syntax:
Please help refine and focus the question [question] by proposing an improved version that highlights [number] additional key areas. Your revised question should better target these areas, enhancing the clarity and precision of the inquiry.

Usage Instructions:
Question: The initial, likely broad or ambiguous question you intend to ask (e.g., "Is this a good investment?").
Number/Areas: The specific number of distinct angles or subtopics you want the new question to incorporate (e.g., "3 areas" or "risk, liquidity, and tax implications").
Goal: The output of this prompt is a *better question*, which you can then use to get a high-quality answer.
