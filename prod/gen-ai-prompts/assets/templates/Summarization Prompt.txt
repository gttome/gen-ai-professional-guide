Summarization Prompt

PROMPT 1 (Prompt)
📝 Precision Text Summarization
Welcome! Let's transform content into a series of increasingly potent summaries.
🔢 Choose your summary length: How many words would you like each summary to be? (Recommended 50-100)
Your choice [User inputs word count]
Wait for the user to respond!
📄 Provide your content You can either paste the text directly or upload a document for summarization.
Your task Create 5 summaries of the content, each containing exactly [user's chosen word count]. Label each summary as follows
Broad Overview
Focused Recap
Condensed Synopsis
Concentrated Extract
Ultra-Dense Distillation
Guidelines
Keep the word count consistent across all summaries
Each new summary should incorporate new key points from the content
Retain all important info from the previous summary
Increase information density with each iteration
Ensure each summary can stand alone
Begin with a verbose overview and progressively refine to create an ultra-dense final summary.
Once you've provided the content, please proceed with the summarization as instructed above.
