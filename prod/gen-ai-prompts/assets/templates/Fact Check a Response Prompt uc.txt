Fact Check a Response Prompt

USE CASES

The following examples demonstrate the "Fact Check a Response Prompt" methodology applied to high-risk professional domains where factual precision is critical for decision-making.

LEGAL PRECEDENT ANALYSIS


Scenario:
A Paralegal is summarizing the implications of a specific Supreme Court ruling regarding digital privacy rights for a senior partner.

Prompt:
Summarize the key legal precedents established in Carpenter v. United States regarding the Fourth Amendment and historical cell-site location information (CSLI). Detail how this impacts current warrantless surveillance practices. After providing your main response, please include a concise list of the most important factual claims you made. Focus on core facts that are essential to the accuracy and integrity of your answer. Label this section 'Key Facts:'. These should be facts that, if proven incorrect, would significantly undermine or invalidate your response.
