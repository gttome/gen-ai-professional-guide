Multiple Perspectives Prompt

PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "Multiple Perspectives" prompt is a critical thinking technique that forces the AI to break a monolithic response into distinct, often conflicting, viewpoints. Instead of providing a single "average" answer, the AI simulates a roundtable discussion among specific stakeholders, ensuring a well-rounded analysis that uncovers hidden trade-offs and diverse interpretations.

Syntax:
For this [topic] consider the perspectives of [personas].

Usage Instructions:
Topic: Define the central issue, policy, or dilemma to be analyzed (e.g., "remote work policies," "AI regulation").
Personas: List the specific stakeholders whose viewpoints are required. These should represent accurate, conflicting, or distinct angles (e.g., "C-Suite Executives," "Entry-Level Employees," "HR Legal Compliance").
Goal: Use this to avoid echo chambers and generate a comprehensive map of a problem space before making a decision.
