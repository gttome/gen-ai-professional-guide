Acting As:

PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "Acting As" prompt enables the AI to adopt a specific role, persona, or character. By using this template, you ensure the AI responds consistently with that chosen identity.

Syntax:
Acting as a [role or persona] and [task].

Usage Instructions:
Role or Persona: Define the specific identity (e.g., "Subject Matter Expert", "Linux Operating System").
Task: Define the specific action, ensuring you provide enough contextual details to help the AI generate accurate responses.
