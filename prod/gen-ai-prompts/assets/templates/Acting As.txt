1. THE PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "Acting As" prompt enables the AI to adopt a specific role, persona, or character[cite: 110]. [cite_start]By using this template, you ensure the AI responds consistently with that chosen identity[cite: 4].

Syntax:
Acting as a [role or persona] and [task][cite: 6].

Usage Instructions:
Role or Persona: Define the specific identity (e.g., "Subject Matter Expert", "Linux Operating System")[cite: 122, 123].
Task: Define the specific action, ensuring you provide enough contextual details to help the AI generate accurate responses[cite: 126, 158].

    A Chief Architect needs to transition a monolithic banking application to a cloud-native environment.
