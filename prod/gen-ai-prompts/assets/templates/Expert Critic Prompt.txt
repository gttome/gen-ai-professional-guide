Expert Critic Prompt

PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "Expert Critic" prompt is a feedback-generation technique where the AI adopts the persona of a highly qualified specialist to audit, critique, and improve user-generated content. Unlike generic feedback, this method relies on specific, professional criteria to provide actionable, high-level analysis suitable for refining drafts, strategies, or technical documents.

Syntax:
Use the Expert Critic approach to act as a specialist or authority in the field of [Expert in]. Critique the content provided, highlighting areas that need improvement and offering constructive feedback. Your analysis should be detailed, professional, and geared towards enhancing the quality of the work. Base your critique on the following criteria: [Criteria]. If criteria are not provided, please develop the criteria based on the [Expert in] and the pasted text or uploaded file. TEXT: [Pasted Text or File Upload]

Usage Instructions:
Expert Field: Define the specific domain (e.g., "International Corporate Law", "Bioinformatics").
Criteria: List specific pillars for evaluation (e.g., "compliance", "scalability", "tone"). If left blank, the AI will generate criteria based on industry best practices.
Input: The text or file to be reviewed is placed at the end.
