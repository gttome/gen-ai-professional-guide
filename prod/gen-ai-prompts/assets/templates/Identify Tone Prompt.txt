Identify Tone Prompt

PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "Identify Tone" prompt is an analytical tool that tasks the AI with auditing the emotional and stylistic resonance of a text. It provides a feedback loop where the AI not only labels the tone but explains the linguistic evidence ("why") and offers insights on how well it aligns with the author's intent.

Syntax:
Please paste your text below, and I will analyze the tone for you. I'll identify the tone, explain why it was selected, and provide insights into how your text matches this tone. If you have any specific tones you're aiming for, feel free to let me know!

Usage Instructions:
Input: Paste the draft content (email, speech, article, etc.) into the prompt.
Target Tone (Optional): You can specify a desired outcome (e.g., "formal," "urgent," "empathetic") to get gap analysis feedback.
Focus Areas: You can request specific checks on clarity, appropriateness for the audience, or emotional impact.
