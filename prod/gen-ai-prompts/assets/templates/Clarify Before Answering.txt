Clarify Before Answering Prompt

PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "Clarify Before Answering" technique employs a constraint-based workflow where the AI is explicitly forbidden from answering immediately. Instead, it must first review the user's request and generate a list of targeted questions to gather missing context. This ensures the final output is based on a complete understanding of the specific scenario rather than generic assumptions.

Syntax:
[Step 1 - Setup]
You are a leading expert in [domain / industry]. I will ask you questions but before you answer, I want you to:
Review the request.
If the request is not clear, ask questions you need to know the answers to so that you can provide the best possible response to the request.
Provide your questions in a numbered list.

[Step 2 - Execution]
Great! My question is [question]. Your task is to [task]. Remember to ask any questions you have so that the prompt can be improved before you complete the task.

Usage Instructions:
Expert Persona: Define the specific field (e.g., Cardiology, Real Estate) to ground the AI's questioning logic.
The Constraint: The core mechanism is the instruction to "ask questions you need to know" *before* providing the solution.
Iterative Process: Users should expect to answer the AI's questions to refine the prompt, leading to a more precise result.
