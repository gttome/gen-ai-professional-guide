Comparative Prompting

PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "Comparative Prompting" technique instructs the AI to evaluate two or more items, concepts, or strategies side-by-side based on specific, user-defined criteria. This forces the AI to structure data in a way that highlights distinctions and trade-offs, aiding in complex decision-making processes.

Syntax:
Provide a comparison based on [factors] for these [entities] in a tabular format.

Usage Instructions:
Factors: Define the specific criteria for analysis (e.g., "cost, scalability, security compliance").
Entities: List the specific items to be compared (e.g., "AWS, Azure, Google Cloud").
Format: The instruction "in a tabular format" is crucial for readability, forcing the AI to organize the data into a clean matrix.
