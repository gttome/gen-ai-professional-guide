Summarization Prompt

USE CASES

The following examples demonstrate the "Summarization Prompt" methodology applied to professional domains where brevity and information density are competing priorities.

EXECUTIVE BOARD REPORTING


Scenario:
A Chief Strategy Officer needs to present a 50-page annual performance report to the Board. They need a summary that captures all financial KPIs without exceeding a single slide's word limit.

Prompt:
[Standard Syntax as above]
Input: [Paste 50-page Annual Report]
Constraint: 100 words.
(Goal: The 'Ultra-Dense Distillation' will likely strip all narrative flow and present a dense block of revenue, EBITDA, and growth percentages suitable for an executive summary.)



ACADEMIC LITERATURE REVIEW


Scenario:
A PhD Candidate is reviewing dozens of dense papers on machine learning architectures. They need to extract the core methodology and results of each paper to populate a comparative matrix.

Prompt:
[Standard Syntax as above]
Input: [Paste Academic Paper on Transformer Architectures]
Constraint: 75 words.
(Goal: The final summary will strip the abstract's introduction and focus purely on the algorithmic novelty, dataset used, and accuracy scores achieved.)



LEGAL DISCOVERY & DEPOSITIONS


Scenario:
A Litigation Associate has a transcript of a 4-hour deposition. They need to extract the key admissions and contradictions for a case strategy memo.

Prompt:
[Standard Syntax as above]
Input: [Paste Deposition Transcript]
Constraint: 150 words.
(Goal: The summary must evolve from a general description of the witness's testimony to a dense list of specific admitted facts and timeline contradictions.)



MARKET INTELLIGENCE ANALYSIS


Scenario:
A Product Manager is analyzing a competitor's new product launch based on press releases, user manuals, and reviews. They need a feature-set comparison.

Prompt:
[Standard Syntax as above]
Input: [Paste Competitor Product Documentation]
Constraint: 80 words.
(Goal: The 'Concentrated Extract' will likely list distinct features and specs (e.g., battery life, processor speed) rather than marketing fluff.)



MEDICAL RESEARCH DIGEST


Scenario:
A Physician needs to stay current with new clinical trials but has limited reading time. They need to understand the inclusion criteria and primary endpoints of a new cardiology study.

Prompt:
[Standard Syntax as above]
Input: [Paste Clinical Trial Protocol or Results]
Constraint: 100 words.
(Goal: The final summary will prioritize p-values, hazard ratios, and adverse event statistics over the study's background rationale.)
