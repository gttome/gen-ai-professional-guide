Expert Critic Prompt

USE CASES

The following examples demonstrate the "Expert Critic" methodology applied to high-stakes professional reviews where precision and authority are required.

CYBERSECURITY COMPLIANCE


Scenario:
A Chief Information Security Officer (CISO) is auditing a draft Incident Response Plan (IRP) for a fintech company to ensure it meets federal standards.

Prompt:
Use the Expert Critic approach to act as a specialist in Information Security and NIST Compliance. Critique the content provided, highlighting areas that need improvement and offering constructive feedback. Your analysis should be detailed, professional, and geared towards enhancing the resilience of the security posture. Base your critique on the following criteria: [alignment with NIST SP 800-61 r2, clarity of escalation chains, forensic data preservation protocols, and legal notification timelines for data breaches].
