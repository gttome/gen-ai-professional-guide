Fact Check a Response Prompt: Prompt Engineering Resource

1. THE PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "Fact Check a Response Prompt" is a self-verification technique designed to combat AI hallucinations. By instructing the AI to isolate and list the "load-bearing" facts of its own argument—those which, if proven false, would invalidate the entire response—the user forces the model to prioritize accuracy and provides a clear checklist for manual verification.

Syntax:
After providing your main response, please include a concise list of the most important factual claims you made. Focus on core facts that are essential to the accuracy and integrity of your answer. Label this section 'Key Facts:'. These should be facts that, if proven incorrect, would significantly undermine or invalidate your response.

Usage Instructions:
Placement: Append this instruction to the end of any complex inquiry where accuracy is paramount.
Mechanism: The AI will generate the standard answer first, followed immediately by the "Key Facts" list.
Verification: The user can then verify just the listed facts against trusted sources, rather than checking every sentence of the generated text.
