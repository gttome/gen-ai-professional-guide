Remove Information Prompt

PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "Remove Information" prompt is a redaction and sanitization technique used to systematically strip specific data points from a text while preserving its overall narrative flow. This is essential for privacy compliance (PII/PHI), confidentiality, or simply tightening communication by removing redundancy.

Syntax:
Thoroughly review the provided information and remove any content specified in the brackets, including [text/category to remove]. Ensure that all instances of the specified content, along with any associated formatting or metadata, are completely eliminated. The final output should be free of the specified content while maintaining the coherence and integrity of the remaining information. Double-check the result to confirm no unintended content has been removed or retained.

Usage Instructions:
Removal Criteria: Define exactly what needs to go inside the brackets (e.g., "personally identifying information," "financial figures," "redundant adverbs").
Integrity Check: The prompt includes an instruction to "maintain coherence," forcing the AI to bridge any gaps left by the deleted text so the result remains readable.
Verification: The "Double-check" instruction adds a self-correction layer to prevent data leakage.
