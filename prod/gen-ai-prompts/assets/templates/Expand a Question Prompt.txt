The Expand a Question prompt helps AI explore user queries in-depth, generating related, focused sub-questions to ensure comprehensive topic coverage. This approach is invaluable in scenarios where user specificity is unclear, aiding structured topic exploration and enhancing responses in various fields.
Using the Expand a Question
The Expand a Question approach is a powerful technique that allows AI to delve deeper into a user’s query by generating a series of related sub-questions. This method helps cover the topic comprehensively, refining the user’s understanding and inquiry direction. By breaking down broad questions into detailed parts, the AI can address each aspect thoroughly. This is particularly useful when users aren't sure about specifics, guiding them through a structured exploration of the topic.
How
Initial Question
Start with a broad question or topic that you need to explore in depth.
Generate Sub-questions
Break down the broad question into several related sub-questions. These should cover different aspects of the main topic.
Answer Sub-questions
Address each sub-question individually to gather detailed information.
Combine Answers
Synthesize the answers to the sub-questions to produce a comprehensive response to the initial broad question.
Why
The Expand a Question approach is effective because it ensures that the AI covers all relevant aspects of a topic, providing a thorough and well-rounded response. It guides the AI to think systematically and comprehensively, making sure that no critical details are overlooked. This method is particularly beneficial for complex topics that require detailed exploration and nuanced understanding. By generating and answering sub-questions, users can achieve a deeper and more precise insight into their original query.
