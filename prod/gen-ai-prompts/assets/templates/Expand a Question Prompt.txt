Expand a Question Prompt

PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "Expand a Question" prompt is a divergence-convergence technique. It instructs the AI not to answer a broad query immediately, but to first break it down into specific sub-questions (divergence), answer those individually, and then synthesize them into a final comprehensive report (convergence). This prevents shallow, generic responses to complex problems.

Syntax:
When tasked with creating a [topic/solution], adhere to the following guidelines:
First, generate a series of additional questions that explore various [subtopic1, subtopic2, …].
After addressing each subtopic through these questions, synthesize the answers to form a comprehensive and cohesive response to the overall question.
This approach ensures that all aspects of the topic are thoroughly covered and integrated into the final answer.

Usage Instructions:
Topic: The broad challenge or goal (e.g., "Designing a new curriculum").
Subtopics: The specific pillars or variables the AI must investigate (e.g., "student retention," "digital accessibility," "industry demand").
Synthesis: The prompt explicitly demands that the final output be a "cohesive response" derived from the sub-answers, not just a list of Q&A.
