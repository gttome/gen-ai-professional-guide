Step by Step Prompt 

PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "Step by Step" prompt (often called Chain of Thought prompting) is a technique used to guide Artificial Intelligence through complex problems by methodically breaking down tasks. While advanced models like GPT-4o have improved reasoning capabilities, this technique remains highly valuable when a user needs to direct the AI through a specific process or method rather than relying on the model's default path. By clearly defining the need for a step-by-step approach, users ensure the AI follows a preferred structure and emphasizes the aspects considered most important.

Syntax:
Let's think about this step by step. [Question or Problem] 

Usage Instructions:
Trigger: Use this phrase when dealing with intricate queries that require a logical progression.
Control: This prompt is particularly useful for enforcing a customized structure or a thorough breakdown of a topic.
Methodology: It directs the AI to process information sequentially, reducing the likelihood of skipping critical dependencies in complex tasks.
