AI Asks You Questions to Form a Response: Prompt Engineering Resource

PROMPT TEMPLATE

The "AI Asks You Questions to Form a Response" technique shifts the interaction from a single query to an iterative dialogue. By instructing the AI to ask targeted questions one at a time, you enable it to gather necessary context and nuance before delivering a final solution.

Syntax:
I would like you to ask me questions to help me [goal/problem]. Continue asking questions until you gather sufficient information to [identify/determine] the [number/criteria] most [outcome]. Ask me one question at a time. Begin by asking the first question.

Usage Instructions:
    Goal/Problem: Clearly state the complex issue or objective you are trying to address (e.g., "diagnose a network failure", "refine a marketing strategy").
    Outcome: Define what the AI should be able to produce after the questioning phase (e.g., "identify the 3 most likely root causes").
    Iterative Constraint: Crucially, instruct the AI to ask "one question at a time." This prevents the AI from overwhelming the user with a list and ensures each question builds on the previous answer.
