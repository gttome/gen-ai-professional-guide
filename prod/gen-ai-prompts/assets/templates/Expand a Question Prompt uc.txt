Expand a Question Prompt

USE CASES

The following examples demonstrate the "Expand a Question" methodology applied to multi-faceted professional challenges where a single-paragraph answer would be insufficient.

URBAN DISASTER RESILIENCE


Scenario:
A City Manager needs a resilience plan for a coastal city facing rising sea levels, but a simple "build walls" answer is too reductive.

Prompt:
When tasked with creating a [comprehensive flood resilience strategy for a coastal metro area], adhere to the following guidelines: First, generate a series of additional questions that explore various [permeable pavement infrastructures, managed retreat legal frameworks, emergency evacuation logistics, and salt-marsh restoration buffers]. After addressing each subtopic through these questions, synthesize the answers to form a comprehensive and cohesive response to the overall question.
