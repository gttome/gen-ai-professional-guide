Multiple Perspectives Prompt

USE CASES

The following examples demonstrate the "Multiple Perspectives" methodology applied to high-complexity professional scenarios where understanding stakeholder friction is essential.

BIOETHICS AND GENETICS


Scenario:
A Policy Institute is drafting a framework for the regulation of human germline editing (CRISPR) and needs to anticipate global objections.

Prompt:
For this [global regulatory framework on human germline editing] consider the perspectives of [clinical geneticists focusing on disease eradication, bioethicists concerned with eugenics, patients with hereditary Huntington's disease, and religious leaders evaluating the moral implications of playing god].



SMART CITY URBAN PLANNING


Scenario:
A City Council is proposing a ban on human-driven vehicles in the central business district to favor autonomous transit, but faces pushback.

Prompt:
For this [municipal proposal to ban human-driven vehicles in the central business district] consider the perspectives of [urban planners focused on congestion reduction, disability rights advocates concerned with accessible transit, logistics companies requiring flexible delivery windows, and civil liberties groups opposing automated surveillance].



CORPORATE GOVERNANCE


Scenario:
A Board of Directors is considering the implementation of a "Poison Pill" strategy to defend against a hostile takeover by an activist investor.

Prompt:
For this [implementation of a shareholder rights plan 'poison pill' defense] consider the perspectives of [the incumbent Board of Directors seeking stability, activist investors seeking short-term alpha, long-term institutional shareholders (pension funds), and employee unions concerned with post-acquisition job security].



ALGORITHMIC POLICING


Scenario:
A Police Department is evaluating the deployment of predictive policing software in high-crime zones.

Prompt:
For this [deployment of predictive policing algorithms in high-crime zones] consider the perspectives of [law enforcement command staff looking for resource optimization, data scientists focused on mitigating training data bias, civil rights attorneys, and community leaders in the affected neighborhoods].



COMMERCIAL SPACE EXPLORATION


Scenario:
An International Space Agency is drafting a treaty regarding the commercial exploitation of asteroid resources.

Prompt:
For this [international treaty on commercial asteroid mining rights] consider the perspectives of [private aerospace CEOs seeking property rights, representatives from developing nations citing the Outer Space Treaty's 'common heritage' clause, environmental scientists concerned with orbital debris, and defense contractors monitoring dual-use technology].
